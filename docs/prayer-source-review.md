# PWG Prayer Source and Permissions Review

This internal tracker records source, wording, permission, and publication-status decisions for named prayers and traditional texts used by PrayWithGod.ai.

## Working Rules

- KJV biblical passages may be shown as copyable prayer text when the exact source passage is identified.
- Modern Bible translations should not be copied unless permission/status is confirmed.
- Historic creeds and traditional prayers should not be displayed as full copyable text until the exact wording source is reviewed.
- When wording is uncertain, PWG may provide educational background and a "Start a Prayer" action instead of full text.
- Keep source notes on public pages clear, modest, and non-overpromising.
